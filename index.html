WORKING HYPOTHESIS · UPDATED

Population background and individual development are modeled separately.

We hypothesize that multigenerational environmental factors impacting endocrine signaling across generations, together with transient perturbations during specific windows of fetal development, can produce alterations in skeletal, cranial, and neurological development that together could result in the phenotype observed in the LB1 fossil recovered from Liang Bua.
Definitive thesis freeze: Version 14 is the prespecified model for the final 500,000-simulation-per-experiment analysis. Once that run is inspected, parameter bounds should not be retuned; any later changes are post-hoc analyses.

POPULATION BASELINE

Flores shifts the broader phenotype distribution closer to LB1.

Although the modern full model produced the lowest single score, the Flores full model had a median distance of 74.04 versus 146.73 and generated D<20 phenotypes about 11.5 times as often.

PATHWAY COMBINATION

IGF + iodine performed better than either pathway alone.

Best Flores scores were 6.38 for IGF, 21.67 for iodine, and 5.87 for IGF + iodine. Iodine therefore behaved primarily as a modifying pathway rather than a sufficient explanation by itself.

INTERGENERATIONAL COMPONENT

The added multigenerational susceptibility effect was small.

Adding the full intergenerational architecture changed the best Flores score only from 5.873 to 5.858. Its global rank sensitivity was also weak relative to developmental skeletal and brain effects.

METHODS & TRANSPARENCY

What is data, what is derived, and what remains assumed?

OBSERVED TARGET

LB1 endpoints

280-mm femur, 430-cc ICV, crural index 83.9, humerofemoral index 87.8, and foot/femur index 70.0.

EMPIRICAL INPUT

Prenatal trajectories

Published fetal femur and ICV trajectories generate growth-velocity weighting functions.

SENSITIVITY PARAMETER

Normalized IGF perturbation

Runs from 0 to 1, where 0 means no modeled perturbation and 1 is the upper edge of the modeled parameter space; it is not a clinical IGF percentage.

SENSITIVITY PRIOR

Flores population baseline

Linear scale 0.64–0.80 and brain exponent 1.6–2.2 are frozen uncertainty ranges, not measured Flores population distributions.

LITERATURE-ANCHORED SENSITIVITY

Intergenerational modifier

Growth-state transmission is constrained to 0.10–0.29 using human maternal–offspring birth-size continuity as an anchor; it is not genetic or proven epigenetic inheritance.

ROBUSTNESS

Correlation-adjusted score

A structural correlation matrix tests whether conclusions survive partial adjustment for shared femur information.

THESIS RULE

No post-result retuning

Once the 500,000-per-experiment run is inspected, parameter changes become explicitly post-hoc analyses.

Scientific scope

A close fit indicates compatibility with the tested physiological architecture. It does not establish that these exposures occurred historically at Liang Bua, nor does the model determine diagnosis, ancestry, or taxonomic status.

VISUAL CONTEXT

Flores, Liang Bua, and the fossil context.

No speculative hominin reconstruction is used.
