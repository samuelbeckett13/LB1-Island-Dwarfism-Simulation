WORKING HYPOTHESIS

Environment, endocrine signaling, and developmental timing.

We hypothesize that multigenerational environmental factors impacting endocrine signaling across generations, together with transient perturbations during specific windows of fetal development, can produce alterations in skeletal, cranial, and neurological development that together result in the phenotype observed in the LB1 fossil recovered from Liang Bua.

INTERPRETATION

Better does not mean proven.

The combined hypothesis moves phenotype space in the predicted direction, but the best validation result still overshoots the selected 430-cc ICV target. The model therefore does not manufacture a perfect LB1 fit.
