WHAT THE MODEL FOUND

Simple mechanisms struggle with the cranial–skeletal differential.

Under the constrained Version 8 framework, none of the physiological analogue models routinely reproduce both the selected 280-mm femur and 430-cc endocranial volume simultaneously.

METHODS & TRANSPARENCY

What is measured, modeled, and uncertain?

OBSERVED TARGET

LB1 measurements

430 cc endocranial volume and 280 mm femur length are the primary selected targets in this version.

EMPIRICAL INPUT

Developmental timing

Published prenatal growth trajectories are used conceptually to weight when tissues are most sensitive to perturbation.

MODEL ASSUMPTION

Physiological coupling

Severity, skeletal coupling, cranial coupling, and persistence are constrained parameters rather than observed LB1 exposures.

INTERPRETATION

Compatibility, not causation

The model tests whether a mechanism is compatible with the selected phenotype. It does not establish diagnosis, ancestry, or taxonomy.

Scientific scope

The site is an interactive research companion to a developmental-physiology model. It deliberately avoids a speculative reconstruction of the LB1 individual and focuses on measurements, mechanisms, model behavior, and archaeological context.
